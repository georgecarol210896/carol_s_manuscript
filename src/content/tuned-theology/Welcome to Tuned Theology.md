## Introduction

Welcome to **Tuned Theology**, a space for careful, unhurried reflection on the Christian faith.

Here you will find writing shaped by the rhythms of the liturgical calendar — Advent, Lent, Easter, Pentecost, and the long stretches of Ordinary Time that make up most of the Christian life.

---

## What to Expect

This section draws from:

- Scripture and biblical theology
- The early church fathers
- Reformed and historical theology
- Catechisms and creeds
- Devotional and spiritual formation traditions

The goal is not novelty but clarity — to think carefully about things that matter most.

---

## Closing Thoughts

Theology is not an academic exercise. It is the slow, faithful work of knowing God and being known by Him. May these reflections serve that end.
